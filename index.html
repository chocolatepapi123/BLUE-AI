Research safety

This app is a research tool. Signals are not execution instructions. Results depend on your imported data and assumptions; the included demo strategy is deliberately simple and is not a validated edge.

Knowledge library

Add transcripts, notes, or text from trading videos. PDF extraction runs in your browser via a CDN-loaded parser; it requires internet access on first load. YouTube links cannot be transcript-fetched directly in a browser due to platform restrictions—paste the transcript alongside the URL.

Market data import

Import OHLC CSV for any supported market. Required columns: timestamp, open, high, low, close. volume is optional. Timestamp should include timezone or use UTC. CSV is stored locally in this browser.

Backtest lab

Demo logic: range sweep + close back inside range, then simple direction confirmation. OHLC intrabar order is ambiguous; engine uses conservative stop-first if SL and TP touch within the same bar. No spreads/slippage are modeled in this browser build.

Signal journal

Signals are generated from the latest imported bars and selected demo rule. No live feed is connected.

Discipline controls

Simple checklist and risk guardrails. Stored locally; they do not execute or block broker orders.
