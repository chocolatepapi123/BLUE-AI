Important limitations

BLUE is a deterministic research tool in this build, not a generative AI or live broker. It only analyzes uploaded candle data. Signal rules are heuristic, and historical results do not guarantee future performance.

Settings & safety

Data handling

BLUE stores knowledge, strategy profiles and uploaded datasets in JSON files under its local data/ folder. Make backups of that folder.

What is included

  • Market CSV parsing and validation
  • Pivot/swing identification, simple liquidity sweep, displacement, three-candle FVG observation
  • Rule-based scan signal
  • Backtest with stop-first assumption for same-candle stop and target hits
  • Knowledge/strategy storage and deterministic research summary

Not included / not automated

  • No live price feed or broker execution
  • No automatic YouTube transcript extraction or AI video understanding
  • No cloud AI model, autonomous learning, or strategy mutation
  • No unattended continuous scanning unless hosted with a separate scheduler
  • Backtest excludes spread, slippage, commission, and precise intrabar path
